Our Story

RAJALAKSHMI CINE DRESSES

Rajalakshmi Cine Dresses is a renowned costume and dress rental store located in the vibrant Triplicane neighborhood of Chennai, Tamil Nadu. With years of experience serving the needs of film productions, cultural events, stage performances, and festive celebrations, we have built a reputation as one of the most reliable and well-stocked cine dress establishments in the city. Our carefully curated collection spans traditional Indian attire, period costumes, folk dress, and theatrical outfits to suit every creative vision.

Situated conveniently at 5/1 Kandappa Street near Irusappa Street in Ice House, Ram Nagar, we welcome customers six days a week from 9:30 AM to 8:30 PM and on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Rated 4.3 out of 5 by over 104 satisfied customers, Rajalakshmi Cine Dresses is committed to quality, affordability, and personalized service. Whether you are a filmmaker, a theatre artist, a student, or a family preparing for a special occasion, we are here to dress you in style.
